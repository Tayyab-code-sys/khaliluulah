The idea in simple words

Many people want to buy things from Afghanistan. But it is hard to find good sellers you can trust. So we want to build a directory. Think of it like a phone book for Afghan suppliers.

  • Buyers use it for free. You just search and find sellers.
  • Sellers pay to list their shop and products.
  • You get contact details fast. No more guessing.
